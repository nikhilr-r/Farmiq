## Field 2: Description
*Copy and paste this directly into the Description text box. (Length: ~1,550 characters — well within LinkedIn's 2,000-character limit).*

```text
Developed AgriQ (internally Farmiq), a multilingual digital platform and AI agronomist designed to bridge the gap between farmers and scientific agricultural resources.

🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture:
• Frontend: React.js, Vite, Tailwind CSS, i18next (Marathi, Hindi, English)
• Backend: Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB (Mongoose ODM)
• AI/ML: Python, PyTorch, FastAPI, timm, Albumentations, Grad-CAM (XAI)

💡 Key Features:
• Crop Doctor (AI Diagnosis): Farmers upload leaf photos to instantly diagnose crop diseases.
• Smart Advisory Engine: Generates real-time, structured advisories (Immediate actions, organic/chemical treatments, prevention strategies, and yield impact).
• Scheme Finder: A geo-mapped directory of State (Maharashtra) and Central government agricultural schemes.
• Crop Knowledge Hub & Calendar: Personalized growth timelines and season-wise crop guides.
• Officer Directory: Localized contact finder mapped to District and Taluka levels.

🔬 Deep Learning Implementation:
• Engineered a weighted ensemble model of EfficientNetV2-S (accuracy-focused) and MobileNetV3-Large (efficiency-focused) in PyTorch.
• Integrated 8-pass Test-Time Augmentation (TTA), Label Smoothing, and Cosine Annealing.
• Achieved a 99.34% Test Accuracy and 0.9923 Macro F1-score across 28 Indian-relevant crop disease classes.
• Leveraged Grad-CAM for Explainable AI (XAI) to highlight exact foliar lesions, boosting user trust.
• Served model predictions via a FastAPI microservice with Apple MPS/CUDA support, achieving an average inference latency of ~124ms.
```
